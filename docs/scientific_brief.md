# MAPLE-SYRUP scientific and development brief

MAPLE-SYRUP lives in `~/MASY`. It is a new orchestration wrapper around actual MAPLE (`~/MAPLE`, wind/shared sediment infrastructure) and SYRUP (`~/SYRUP`, water extension importing `maple_syrup`). The wrapper uses `masy` to avoid colliding with SYRUP's existing package/distribution names. Preserve independent upstream development and shared bed ownership.

The user has explicitly prioritized wind-water connection for collaboration. Finish the first small alternating evolving-landscape experiment before broad hydraulic improvements. Existing SYRUP hydraulic limitations carry into the combined model and must be declared, not disguised as newly solved physics. Start with supported small geometry, explicit boundaries and ordinary rainfall; Chastre's no-spill closed-boundary stress case is not the initial integration demo.

Wind and water events never transport simultaneously. MAPLE executes wind physics, including its normal feedback and cache behavior; SYRUP executes conservative water physics, including recession, actual MAPLE inventory exchange and explicit event-end dry reset. MASY supplies forcing/event order, transfers the same bed, and records joint accounting. Calling stock standalone case CLIs sequentially is insufficient if they reinitialize the landscape.

The schedule initially means sequential events. A wind event has duration and regional friction/shear velocity `u_*` in m/s plus MAPLE's direction convention (clockwise from north). Rain is an explicit piecewise series in mm/h and seconds, with optional bound spatial scaling. Water events include physical recession after rain ends. Global time advances by actual elapsed process time; event-local clocks are mapped explicitly. Fixed-calendar overlaps require a declared policy and cannot silently shift or truncate forcing.

A water handoff requires genuine `DryHandoff`, empty/resolved mobile load, valid bed/accounting and documented external removal of residual surface/soil water under the current dry-again assumption. Wind event boundaries also require a verified treatment of airborne material: zero input wind alone does not demonstrate empty cohorts. Never erase either process's sediment. No initial-case reload or fixed-composition legacy replay at transitions.

Exchange complete class-resolved voxels/active layer, availability, physical and committed surfaces, storage references and relevant prescribed surface properties. Separate authoritative physical fields from process-derived caches. Restart must include active-process state, schedule cursor and clocks, provenance and references, with no duplicated accounting. Do not assume windowed SYRUP columns can enter the current MAPLE wind scheduler; inspect and prove that interface. A dense small CPU milestone is acceptable if its restricted scope is explicit.

Latest versions are selected at the start of each new run, checked together and locked to exact actual source manifests for that run. Both normally share one compatible MAPLE runtime. Current SYRUP has source-bound private/windowed/storage interfaces; these are compatibility work, not permission to bypass guards. Latest arbitrary revisions cannot be promised automatically compatible. Record incompatibilities clearly, qualify narrow upstream improvements, and keep existing runs/restarts on their original pair. No silent historical fallback, mutable mid-run dependency, copied physics or auto-pull.

Water routing keeps the net-elevation-only rule and MAPLE threshold, including across wind-driven terrain changes. Wind resumes with its own appropriate cache invalidation after water changes. Vegetation is shared landscape state: the [information plan](vegetation_information_plan.md) reserves versioned between-event updates and joint changes to native soil water, including future uptake of stored water. The qualified prescribed/reset profile is preserved. Growth/mortality/uptake laws and any retained-soil-water completion policy require the user's scientific direction before implementation; full ecohydrology/ET, nutrients and direct dry-cell splash remain deferred. Water conservation and per-class sediment conservation use upstream tolerances. Preserve actual values, avoid full-volume copies and aim for resident CPU/GPU handoff.

Automatic Codex-Claude mode remains preferred. Read AGENTS.md and the handoff first; work only in the assigned scope and preserve logs. Claude leaves implementation changes uncommitted; Codex independently verifies acceptance and may publish accepted milestones under the user's October19 authorization. Relevant source/findings may be shared for review; saved simulation payloads remain local unless separately authorized. Use existing MAPLE environments, ordinary permissions and no unattended simulation/GPU work merely because instructions exist. Do not modify MAPLE/SYRUP or active imported files without an explicitly scoped task.

See `docs/architecture.md` for contracts, `docs/upstream_interface_audit.md` for observed API candidates/blockers, and `docs/implementation_plan.md` for bounded milestones. Setup is not proof of an executable coupled model.
